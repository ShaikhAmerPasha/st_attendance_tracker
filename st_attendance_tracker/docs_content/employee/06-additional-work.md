# Additional Work

Use this page to record work outside your normal workday: extra hours, a weekend, or a day you were not checked in.

![Additional Work form](/assets/st_attendance_tracker/docs/c_additional.png)

## Log additional work

1. **Work date \*:** pick the day the work happened. It can be today or any earlier day, even if that day's checkout is already submitted.
2. **Login time \*** and **Logout time \*:** when you started and finished, for example `9:30 AM`. Hours are calculated for you.
3. Add tasks:
   - **Task description:** what you did.
   - **Task Name / Project:** group the work under a project name.
   - **Status:** for example *Completed*.
   - **Actual time:** time spent, such as `1h 30m`.
   - **Add task** adds more rows. **Add project** adds another project group.
4. Press **Submit additional work** (bottom bar).

The bottom bar shows how many tasks are in the entry.

## Right panel

- **This week / This month** – total additional hours.
- **Completed / Pending** – task counts.
- **Total entries** – number of additional-work entries.
- **Recent entries** – your latest submissions.

## History

Earlier entries are listed under **History**. When you have none, the page says *No additional work logged yet.*

## Tips

- Submit the entry the same day so times are accurate.
- Use a clear project name so reviewers understand the work.
- Additional work does not change your normal check-in record.

# Getting Started

ST Attendance Tracker records when you start and finish work, and the tasks you do each day. This guide uses the **Classic design**. Everything described here is also available in the calendar design, which you can switch to from the **New design** button.

## What you can do

| Menu item | What it is for |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Today** | Daily Check-in: plan tasks, check in, work, check out |
| **My history** | Past days, check-in and check-out times, tasks |
| **Recurring** | Tasks that are added to your day automatically |
| **Task Backlog** | Work for later, not counted in today's tasks |
| **Additional work** | Extra hours outside your normal day |

## Finding your way around

![Daily Check-in, Classic design](/assets/st_attendance_tracker/docs/c_checkin_full.png)

1. **Top bar:** page name, today's date, **Docs** (this guide), **Tour** (a guided walk-through of the current page), **New design** (switch layout), the notification bell and your account menu (initials).
2. **Profile card (top left):** your name, department and a status badge such as *Not checked in*.
3. **Navigation (left):** the five pages above. Numbers beside *Today*, *Recurring* and *Task Backlog* show how many items are waiting.
4. **Main area (centre):** the work area of the page you opened.
5. **Summary (right):** today's counts and progress by project.

## Your day in four steps

1. **Plan:** on *Today*, add the tasks you intend to do.
2. **Check in:** choose login time and work location, then press **Check in & start day**.
3. **Work:** mark tasks Completed or In progress and record the time taken.
4. **Check out:** press **Check out & submit** when you finish.

See *Daily Check-in*, *Managing Your Tasks* and *Checking Out* for each step in detail.

## Account menu

Click your initials (top right) to open your profile, switch between the **Classic** and **calendar** design, go to the ERP desk, or log out.

## Who can see my data?

You see only your own records. Your Team Leader sees your attendance and tasks, and HR and Management see everyone's. Keep task descriptions clear and accurate.

# Team Dashboard (My team)

For Team Leaders. See who has checked in today, who is late or missing, and what each person is working on. **My team** appears in your left menu only when people report to you.

![Team Dashboard, Classic design](/assets/st_attendance_tracker/docs/c_team.png)

## Header and date

The red header shows your name, department and role (*Team Leader*). Use the **Date** box to look at any past day.

## Summary cards

| Card | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Total** | People in your team |
| **Checked in** | Started their day |
| **Checkout done** | Submitted their day |
| **Missing** | Not checked in yet |
| **Late** | Checked in after the late threshold |

## Filter chips

**All**, **Checked in**, **Late**, **Missing**, **On leave** and **Checkout done** narrow the list below. Click a chip again or choose **All** to reset.

## Team member cards

Each card shows the person's initials, name, check-in status (for example *Not checked in*) and a coloured badge such as **Missing**. Click a card, or its arrow, to expand it.

![Expanded team member](/assets/st_attendance_tracker/docs/c_team_open.png)

An expanded card shows:

- **Login** and **Logout** times,
- **Tasks** for the selected date, with status,
- an **Assign task** button (see *Assigning Tasks to Your Team*).

## Detail panel (right)

Selecting a person fills the dark panel on the right with their **Task summary** (Completed, Pending, Total) and **Time** (Login, Logout). Until you pick someone it reads *Select an employee*.

## Good habits

- Open the dashboard each morning and follow up with **Missing** people.
- Check **Late** and **Checkout done** counts at the end of the day.
- Use the date box to review earlier days before one-to-one meetings.

# FAQ and Troubleshooting

**I cannot check in.**
Add at least one task first. If WFH is selected, you need an approved Attendance Request for that day.

**The Work location menu has only one option.**
Saturdays are WFH for everyone, and Remote employees are fixed to Remote.

**It says Late.**
You logged in after the company late threshold. Set the real login time and add a remark if needed.

**I forgot to check out.**
Request HR approval from the Today page. Only your last working day can be requested.

**I cannot edit a task.**
Tasks on past days and after checkout are locked.

**A task keeps coming back every day.**
It is either a recurring template (change it on the *Recurring* page) or an unfinished task being carried over. Untick **Carry forward**, finish it, or move it to the backlog.

**Where did my backlog task go?**
Open *Task Backlog*. If you moved it to today, it is on the Today page.

**Which design am I using?**
Use the **New design** or **Classic design** button in the top bar or account menu. Both do the same things.

**Who do I ask for help?**
Your Team Leader first, then HR.

# Daily Check-in

The **Today** page is where every working day starts. Until you check in, the page shows a *Not checked in* badge and a **Check in & start day** button.

![Daily Check-in page before checking in](/assets/st_attendance_tracker/docs/c_checkin_full.png)

## Top strip

Five figures summarise your day and update as you work:

- **Working hours** – your working hours for the day, shown once you are checked in.
- **Tasks overview** – tasks completed out of the total (for example *0/8 Completed*).
- **Check-in time** – the login time you selected.
- **Work mode** – Office, WFH, Remote or Hybrid.
- **Total task hours** – the sum of the time you record on tasks.

The header also shows a progress bar, your net hours and task count.

## Step 1 – Login time and work location

1. **Login time \*** (required): defaults to the current time. Change it only if you actually started earlier. Logins after the company's late threshold are marked **Late**.
2. **Work location:** the choices depend on your work type.

| Your work type | Choices |
|----------------|---------|
| Office | Office, or WFH if you have an approved *Attendance Request* for the day |
| Remote | Remote (fixed) |
| Hybrid | Office or Hybrid on your home days. On office days, WFH needs an approved *Attendance Request* |
| Saturday | WFH for everyone (fixed) |

If WFH is blocked, a message explains that you need an approved Attendance Request. Ask your HR contact to approve it first.

## Step 2 – Add today's tasks

You must plan at least one task before you can check in.

- **Recurring** tasks from your templates appear in their own section with a *Recurs daily* (or weekday) badge. Add an estimate if you want one.
- **Carried from previous days** shows unfinished work from earlier days, grouped by project, with a *Carried* badge and a *Day N* counter.
- **+ Task** (bottom bar, or press **Shift+T**) adds a new task row. Type a **Task description**, and optionally an estimate such as `1h 30m` or `45m`.
- **Project** (bottom bar, or press **Shift+P**) creates a project group. Tasks inside a project are listed together and counted in *Project progress* on the right.
- **Add task to this project** adds another task to an existing group.
- The **x** on a task removes it. The **x** on a project removes the project and all its tasks.

## Step 3 – Check in

Press **Check in & start day**. The page changes to the working view, your status badge shows *Checked in*, and the working-hours timer starts. The bottom bar now shows **Check out & submit**.

## Right panel

**Today's summary** counts Completed, In progress, Pending and Total tasks. **Project progress** shows how many tasks of each project are done, for example *0/2*.

## Tips

- Plan the night before or first thing in the morning. A planned list makes checkout quick.
- Estimates are optional but help your Team Leader see workload.
- Use the **Tour** button for an on-screen walk-through.

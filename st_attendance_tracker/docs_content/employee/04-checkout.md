# Checking Out

Check out at the end of your day to submit your attendance and tasks.

## Normal checkout

1. Update every task: status, **Actual time** and any remark.
2. Press **Check out & submit** in the bottom bar.
3. Read the confirmation: *Check out for today?* It warns that task statuses and time can no longer be edited.
4. Press **Yes, check out**. (Press **No, go back** to keep working.)

After checkout your net hours are calculated, your tasks are locked, and your Team Leader and HR can see the finished day in **My history**.

## What happens to unfinished tasks?

Pending and In-progress tasks are **carried forward** to your next working day and appear under *Carried from previous days*. Untick **Carry forward** on a task before checkout if you do not want it carried.

## Missed the checkout window

Checkout closes at a cutoff time set by HR. If it has passed, the page shows a lock message such as *Checkout closed at … Get late checkout approval from HR.*

1. Type an optional reason, for example *Power cut at office*.
2. Press **Request HR approval**.
3. The page shows *Approval requested. HR has been notified.*
4. When HR approves, refresh the page. You can now check out for that date.

You can request approval only for your most recent working day.

## Tips

- Check out before the cutoff so you never need approval.
- Record **Actual time** as you finish tasks, not at the end of the day.

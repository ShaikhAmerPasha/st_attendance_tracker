# My History

Your personal attendance record, newest day first.

![My history, Classic design](/assets/st_attendance_tracker/docs/c_history.png)

## Day cards

Each card shows:

- the **date** tile and weekday (for example *Tue, Oct 13*),
- **check-in time**, **check-out time** and **net hours**,
- a **tasks done** count (for example *2/10*) with a progress bar,
- a status badge.

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Late** | You checked in after the late threshold |
| **No checkout** | The day was never submitted with a checkout |
| **Partial** | Some, but not all, tasks were completed |
| **All done** | Every task was completed |

A day with checkout but no completed tasks shows no completion badge.

## Open a day

Click a card (or the arrow on its right) to expand it.

![Expanded day](/assets/st_attendance_tracker/docs/c_history_open.png)

You will see:

- **Login** (with a *Late* tag if relevant), **Logout** and **Net hours**,
- **Tasks**: each with its project, a *Carried* tag if it came from an earlier day, and **Time taken for task completion**,
- a state: **Done**, or **Rolled over** when the task moved to the next day.

## Tips

- Use *No checkout* badges to spot days that need a late-checkout request or HR correction.
- You cannot edit a submitted day. Ask your Team Leader or HR if something is wrong.

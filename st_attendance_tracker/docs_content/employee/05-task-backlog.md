# Task Backlog

A parking area for work you will do later. Backlog tasks **never count** towards today's pending tasks until you move them to today.

![Task Backlog, Classic design](/assets/st_attendance_tracker/docs/c_backlog.png)

## Summary cards

- **In backlog** – tasks waiting with no target date.
- **Stale** – tasks older than 14 days. Review these first.
- **Today** – tasks already on today's list.

## The backlog list

Each row shows the description, its project (for example *Training plan*) and a red badge such as *32 days in backlog*. Controls on each row:

- **Arrow (→)** moves the task to today's list.
- **Bin** deletes the task.
- **Drag handle (dotted grip)** lets you drag the row.

## Move a task to today

- Click the **arrow** on the row, **or**
- **Drag** the row onto **Today preview** on the right.

The task then appears in your Daily Check-in list.

## Add a backlog task

Use **Task** (or **Project**) in the bottom bar, type a description and save. Tasks you move with **Move to backlog** from the Daily Check-in page also land here, as do tasks your Team Leader adds for you.

## Right panel

**Today's summary** shows completed and total tasks. **Today preview** lists what is already on today's list so you can judge whether you have room for more.

## Tips

- Clear the *Stale* count every week.
- Keep descriptions specific so you remember the context.

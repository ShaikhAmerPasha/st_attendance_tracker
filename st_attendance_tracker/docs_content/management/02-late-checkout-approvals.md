# Late Checkout Approvals

Checkout closes at a daily cutoff. An employee who missed it must ask HR for approval before they can submit their day.

## The process

1. The employee sees a lock message on Today and presses **Request HR approval**, with an optional reason.
2. The request appears in **Late Checkout Requests** on the Management Dashboard (visible to **HR Manager** only), showing the employee, date and reason.
3. Review the request.
4. Type an **approval note**. This is required.
5. Press **Approve**.
6. The employee refreshes and can complete checkout for that date.

If the panel shows *No pending requests*, nothing is waiting.

## Rules

- Until the missed checkout is completed, the employee **cannot check in** on the next working day. Approving quickly unblocks them.

- Only the employee's most recent working day can be requested.
- A note is mandatory and is kept for audit.
- If saving fails, the button changes to **Retry**.

## Tips

- Approve promptly. The employee cannot check in for the new day until the missed day is closed.
- Write meaningful notes such as *Verified with team leader*.

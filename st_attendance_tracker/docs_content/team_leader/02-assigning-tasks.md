# Assigning Tasks to Your Team

Give a team member work directly from **My team**.

## Steps

1. Open **My team** in the left menu.
2. Click the person's card to expand it.
3. Press **Assign task** (beside *Tasks*).

![Assign a task dialog](/assets/st_attendance_tracker/docs/c_assign.png)

4. Choose when it should appear:
   - **Assign for today:** the task shows in their Daily Check-in list now.
   - **Add to backlog:** it goes to their Task Backlog for later.
5. Enter a **Description \*** (required): say what to do and what done looks like.
6. Optionally add a **Project** and an **Estimated time** such as `1h 30m`.
7. Press **Assign task**. Use **Cancel** or the **x** to close without saving.

## What the employee sees

The task appears in their list under the project you chose. They update its status and record the time. Their progress is visible on your dashboard.

## Tips

- Write clear descriptions. The employee may read them on a phone.
- Add an estimate to help the employee plan their day.
- Use **Add to backlog** for work without a deadline.
- You can also use **Create → Assign team task** in the calendar design.

## Your own tasks

Team Leaders also check in, plan tasks, check out and use backlog, recurring tasks and additional work like any employee. See the **Employee Guide**.
